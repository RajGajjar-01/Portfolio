A billing workspace for small Indian businesses. Set up your company once, keep
customers and inventory on file, and turn out invoices, delivery challans, and
proformas from reusable templates. Each one renders as a PDF and goes straight
to the customer by email or WhatsApp. It's now live as UnifiedDesk.

## What it does

- **Invoices built for GST**: HSN/SAC codes filled in from inventory, tax and discount per line item, round-off, and the amount in words using Indian numbering (lakhs and crores).
- **More than invoices**: delivery challans and proformas use the same templates and line items.
- **Sending**: PDF generation, then delivery by Gmail or WhatsApp, with a compose window and a searchable customer picker.
- **Data tables**: build your own tables with configurable columns, reminders, and export.
- **Accounts**: email/password or Google sign-in. Claims-based JWTs live in cookies, so routes scoped to one owner skip a database lookup.

## Security and ops

Bank details are encrypted at rest with AES-256-GCM and masked in the UI.
Rate limiting runs on Redis, with an in-memory fallback if Redis can't be
reached. Every response carries security headers, and each log line from
structlog includes the request ID. CI runs lint, type checks, mock tests, and
integration tests against real Postgres and Redis.

## Deploy

The [FastAPI backend](https://github.com/RajGajjar-01/AutoInvoice-backend)
runs as a container on AWS Lambda behind Mangum, using Supabase Postgres and
Upstash Redis. The React frontend runs on Cloudflare Workers. The Worker also
proxies `/api` so the auth cookies count as first-party. Without that, Safari's
tracking protection dropped the cross-site cookies from the Lambda URL, and
iOS users kept getting sent back to the login page.
